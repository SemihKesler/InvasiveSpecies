<p align="center">
  <strong>Forest Defenders</strong> is a virtual reality experience developed in Unity with the goal to educate viewers about processes used to exterminate invasive plants and trees harming Illinois ecosystems. Approximately a third of plants in the state’s ecosystems are invasive, often unnoticed by the general public. While exploring the VR environment, an imaginary Illinois forest, participants have the opportunity to utilize manual and chemical control methods to eliminate invasive species, restoring order to the environment. The experience aims to promote community involvement in the control of invasive species, especially at local levels. The project was exhibited at CAVE2 in the University of Illinois Chicago’s (UIC) Electronic Visualization Laboratory (EVL).
